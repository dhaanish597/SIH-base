App-wide Background Theme

You want the whole app to look like the PNG — deep blue celestial sky, not flat black. How far should this go?

A

Roadmap pages only

The fantasy starfield/nebula background only appears on the 3 roadmap pages (subject map, chapter snake, topic panel). All other pages keep the current dark theme. Least disruptive, ships fastest.

B

Full app — global CSS swap

Replace the flat #07070F background globally with a deep midnight-blue nebula gradient + subtle star field. Every page gets the new feel. All existing cards/nav sit on top unchanged — only the base background changes.

C

Full app — per-section themed

Each major section gets its own biome: Math = violet nebula, Science = teal aurora, History = amber dusk, etc. Immersive but complex to maintain and coordinate across pages.
