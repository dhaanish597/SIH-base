Diversity in Plants and Animals Around Us

The natural world is filled with a vast variety of plants and animals. Each living thing has unique features that make it special.

"Trees stand in the Sun and give shade to others. Their fruits are also for others. Likewise, good people bear all hardships and bring welfare to others."

In a simple nature walk, one can observe different types of plants — from grasses to tall trees, and a variety of animals including birds, butterflies, monkeys, and more. Each bird has a distinct chirp, reflecting the amazing diversity of nature.

Try observing the plants and animals around for their unique characteristics like size, shape, color, and habits.

Grouping of Plants

Plants can be grouped based on features like height, stem type, and leaf arrangement.

  • Herbs: Soft, green stems, usually short (e.g., Tomato plant)
  • Shrubs: Medium height, woody stems that branch near the ground (e.g., Rose plant)
  • Trees: Tall plants with hard, thick woody stems (e.g., Mango tree)
  • Climbers and Creepers: Plants needing support or spreading on ground

Grouping of Animals

Animals can be grouped by their movement and body parts used for movement.

  • Flying animals: Use wings to fly (e.g., Pigeon, Housefly)
  • Walking animals: Use legs to walk or run (e.g., Ant, Goat)
  • Swimming animals: Use fins to swim (e.g., Fish)
  • Amphibians: Can live in water and on land (e.g., Frog)

Adaptations and Habitats

Plants and animals adapt to their surroundings. Their special features help them survive in diverse environments like deserts, mountains, oceans, and forests.

  • Desert: Plants like cactus have thick, fleshy stems for water storage; camels have wide hooves for walking on sand.
  • Mountains: Trees like deodar have sloping branches to shed snow, and rhododendrons vary their height to survive wind.
  • Oceans: Fish have streamlined bodies and fins for swimming.

Conservation and Biodiversity

Biodiversity is important for a healthy planet. Loss of habitat leads to decline in species like Bengal tigers and cheetahs. Conservation efforts and protected areas help preserve this diversity.

Community protection of sacred groves and government projects like Project Tiger contribute to protecting biodiversity.
